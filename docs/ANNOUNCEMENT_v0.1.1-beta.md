# Announcement drafts for v0.1.1-beta

## Discord
**Golden Sun - Dual Screen Companion v0.1.1 (beta) is out!** 🌞
What's new:
- **Android app (beta)**: the same companion UI on phones, tablets and **dual-screen handhelds** (game on one screen, the companion on the other, ZL swaps them). Controller support, touch buttons you can size / hide / vibrate, saves you can export and import, fast forward up to 16x.
- **Back to the battle**: after peeking at another tab in a fight, one tap on the footer returns to the battle panel (3DS and Android).
- **The Lost Age** fixes: no more four Isaacs when you start a new game; Equip best now finds better gear; most areas have real names.
- Sound rework on Android (the crackle is gone).

Get it from the latest release: `GoldenSun3DS_Patcher_v0.1.1-beta.zip` (3DS, builds the .cia / .3ds from your own ROM) and `GoldenSunDualScreen_v0.1.1-beta.apk` (Android).
No ROMs are included, bring your own. It is a beta: please report what breaks (the testing checklist is in `docs/TESTING.md`, and on Android Menu > Settings > Copy debug info makes the report easy). Thanks for testing!

## Reddit (r/3dshacks / r/GoldenSun / r/AndroidGaming; check each subreddit's rules first)
**Title:** Golden Sun - Dual Screen Companion v0.1.1 beta: touch companion for Golden Sun / The Lost Age on 3DS, now with an Android app for dual-screen handhelds

**Body:**
This is a fan project (not affiliated with Nintendo / Camelot) that runs Golden Sun and The Lost Age from your own ROMs with a touch companion UI on the second screen: party, Psynergy, Djinn, a live map drawn from your ROM, items, battle info, tap-to-equip and tap-to-cast through the game's own menus.

v0.1.1 adds:
- An **Android app (beta)** that runs the same code: portrait, landscape, controllers, and dual-screen handhelds such as the Retroid Pocket Duo (game on one display, companion on the other, swappable).
- Back-to-battle footer, Lost Age fixes (party list in the opening, Equip best, area names), Android settings (touch buttons, 16x fast forward, export / import saves) and a sound fix.

It needs your own legally obtained ROMs; none are included. The 3DS side targets a New 3DS XL (Old 3DS runs slowly). Feedback and bug reports are very welcome, especially from other dual-screen devices and from real 3DS hardware.
